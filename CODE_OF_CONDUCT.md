# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MOONATLAS Science to be a place where anyone can check a scientific claim, disagree with it in public,
and be treated with respect while doing so. Everyone participating — contributors, reviewers, maintainers and
people opening issues — is expected to follow this document.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and respectful about the person.
- Disagree with evidence: a derivation, a measurement, an upstream source, a failing test.
- Accept that "I was wrong" is a normal outcome of a scientific review, for contributors and maintainers alike.
- Assume competence and good faith, including across languages and time zones.
- Keep provenance honest: say what you verified, what you assumed and what you did not check.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks; demeaning comments about background, identity or expertise.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions without new evidence, brigading a review, or spamming.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Presenting fabricated, unverifiable or AI-generated results as measured values; silently altering published
  data or provenance. In a scientific repository, this is the most serious violation.
- Using the project to imply endorsement by NASA, IBM or any organisation that has not given it.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code and commit messages) and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub's report feature or by contacting a maintainer through the address
published on the repository profile. Reports are handled discreetly; the reporter's identity is not shared
without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban a participant. Actions are proportionate: a first, non-malicious violation is normally met with
a private explanation.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith can be reported the same way and face the same
consequences.
